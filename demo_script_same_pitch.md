# Same Pitch, Different Soul — camera read

*Read it as one breath. Don't change your pitch — weave the voices in wherever it
feels right. Bring the last line back in your own voice so it buttons warm.*

This is my voice — the real one, right where it always lives but watch what happens when I borrow
somebody else. Same pitch, same breath — different person entirely.

Every other tool makes you chase the model. Mine doesn't ask me to become anyone — it reads a voice's whole range straight off its weights and comes to find *me*, right where I already am. So I stay me — the pitch, the cadence, the little chaos — while the face keeps changing underneath. One voice in; anyone you want, out. And I never once had to leave home.
