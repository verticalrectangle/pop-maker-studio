# User Scenarios

## Jacob the Chud

Drags in a 6ix9ine snippet he downloaded from a YouTube ripper. Immediately clicks "Rave" because it has random positions and neon chaos. Cranks font size to max. Doesn't touch anything else. Exports at 1080p, posts to his finsta, captions it "🔥🔥🔥". Comes back the next day, does the same thing with a Ken Carson leak. Never discovers the karaoke mode. Uses the Glitch preset once, calls it "cyberpunk aesthetics" to his friends.

---

## Sally the Normie

Opens it, sees "Spotify" is selected by default, clicks generate. Watches the text appear, nods. Goes to font size slider, nudges it slightly larger. Spends 11 minutes picking between "Cottagecore" and "Lo-fi" for her Gracie Abrams edit. Lands on Lo-fi. Exports vertical. Posts to TikTok with a brown filter on top. Gets 4,200 views. Tells her friend about it, describes it as "this little app that does the lyrics automatically." Never exports above 720p because she doesn't notice the quality dropdown.

---

## Sarah the Influencer

Has a brand deal with a supplement company. Opens Pop Maker Studio for her "aesthetic morning routine" reel. Spends 3 minutes on the preset grid not reading the taglines, just looking at the color chips in the corner. Picks "Apple" because it looks clean and she associates the name with premium. Doesn't like that it's one word at a time — switches to "Spotify" because she's literally seen it on Spotify.

Exports, opens the file, decides the font is too small. Goes back, drags the font size slider all the way to the right, exports again. Posts it. The text is now comically huge and covers her face in half the shots. Gets 180k views because people are quoting it in the comments saying "babe the words" and she reposts it thinking they're complimenting her.

DMs the developer asking if there's a way to make it "more rose gold."

Has a folder on her desktop called `FINAL_EXPORT_v7_REAL_USE_THIS.mp4`.

## Harry the Hoodlum

Doesn't download it, his boy sent him the folder over Discord. Runs the binary straight from Downloads. Drags in an unreleased drill track that he definitely shouldn't have. Clicks "Drill" preset immediately without looking at anything else because he saw the name. Doesn't know what WhisperX is, just hits the transcribe button and walks away to roll up.

Comes back, half the lyrics are wrong because the mic quality on the track is genuinely terrible — it transcribed "on the block" as "on the flock" and "bro got hit" as "pro got it". Doesn't fix them. Exports anyway.

The video has a watermark from a different app he forgot he had installed. Sends the exported file back to the same Discord, someone replies "🔥" and someone else replies "fix the lyrics fam." Harry says "it's a demo."

Never opens it again. Two weeks later asks his boy "what was that lyric video ting you sent me" and his boy sends him back this exact same folder.

## Jerry the Gen Xer

Finds out about it because his 19 year old nephew mentioned it at Christmas. Writes down the name on a Post-it note. Loses the Post-it note. Finds it three weeks later stuck to the back of his work laptop.

Downloads it, gets a terminal error because he's missing a dependency. Googles the error, lands on a Stack Overflow post from 2019, follows the instructions, breaks something else. Spends 45 minutes fixing that. Gets it running. Feels genuinely accomplished.

Drags in a Steely Dan song he ripped from his own CD in 2008. Hits transcribe. Waits patiently, doesn't touch anything, reads the whole UI while it loads. Is the only person who has ever read the taglines on the preset cards. Picks "Film" because it says "classic subtitle" and that makes sense to him.

Thinks the result looks great. Shows his wife. His wife says "that's neat." He exports it, puts it in a folder called `Steely Dan Lyric Video Project` on his desktop next to seventeen other folders that all say `Project` in the name.

Emails you a bug report with full steps to reproduce, his OS version, and a screenshot. The bug report is completely correct. You fix it in twenty minutes. He never sees the fix because he doesn't update.

## Garry the Gooner

Has 47 tabs open. One of them is Pop Maker Studio's GitHub page, has been open for 6 weeks. Finally downloads it at 2am because he can't sleep.

Drags in a Bladee track. Transcription finishes. Doesn't touch the preset, just stares at the preview for four minutes watching the words flash by. Opens the typography panel, hovers over every single preset card reading the hover preview without clicking any of them. This is the most interaction he will have with the app tonight.

Clicks "Cyberpunk." Watches it. Clicks "Neon." Watches it. Clicks back to "Cyberpunk." Decides he needs to find the perfect font before he can commit to anything. Googles whether the app supports custom fonts. It doesn't. Closes it.

Opens it again the next night. Drags in a different Bladee track. Goes directly to "Cyberpunk." Exports. Watches the export progress bar the entire time without moving. File comes out perfect.

Never posts it. It lives in his Downloads folder. He watches it locally sometimes.

Tabs still open.
